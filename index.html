Eligibility & Engineering Notes

    Solar System Differences

    Choosing the right solar system depends on your budget, energy goals, and power reliability needs:

    • On-Grid Systems: Maximizes financial savings and ROI by exporting excess generation back to the utility grid via net metering. Shuts down during grid cuts for safety.
    • Off-Grid Systems: Completely independent from the utility grid. Relies entirely on battery storage to power day and night loads.
    • Hybrid / Semi-Hybrid Systems: The best of both worlds. Active net-metering allows grid export for high savings, while integrated battery banks provide uninterrupted backup during utility cuts.

    🏢 Multi-Customer Building Solar & PM Surya Ghar (Category B)

    MERC VNM / GNM

    Comprehensive guidelines for implementing shared rooftop solar in apartments and co-operative housing societies (CHS):

    • Shared Rooftop Array with Individual Inverters / Virtual Net Metering: Individual flat owners share the building's common terrace while generating solar power directly offset against their personal MSEDCL electricity bills.
    • PM Surya Ghar Category B Direct Bank Subsidy: Each residential flat is eligible for individual direct-to-bank subsidy under pro-rata slabs (₹30,000/kWp up to 2 kWp, ₹18,000/kWp for 2–3 kWp, max cap ₹78,000 for ≥3 kWp) up to the building cap of min(3 kWp × Number of Flats, 500 kWp).
    • Mandatory MNRE 07.06.2024 Compliance: 100% DCR ALMM modules are mandatory. Elevated structures with ≥8 ft ground clearance preserve terrace usability and qualify for full subsidy. Capex mode only (consumer-funded / bank loan). Ground-mounted and off-grid systems are ineligible. Eligible Maharashtra DISCOMs include MSEDCL, BEST, Adani Electricity, and Tata Power.
    • Common Facility Loads (Category C): Common area meters (lifts, water pumps, corridor lighting, EV charging) receive separate society subsidy at flat ₹18,000/kWp (capped at min(kWp, 3 × Flats - Resident CFA, 500 kWp)). Power must strictly serve common services and cannot supply individual residential flats.
