Eligibility & Engineering Notes

    Solar System Differences

    Choosing the right solar system depends on your budget, energy goals, and power reliability needs:

    • On-Grid Systems: Maximizes financial savings and ROI by exporting excess generation back to the utility grid via net metering. Shuts down during grid cuts for safety.
    • Off-Grid Systems: Completely independent from the utility grid. Relies entirely on battery storage to power day and night loads.
    • Hybrid / Semi-Hybrid Systems: The best of both worlds. Active net-metering allows grid export for high savings, while integrated battery banks provide uninterrupted backup during utility cuts.
